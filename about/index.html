About

Technologist and Creative, On Purpose.

I have always lived in two worlds at once. I am a technologist: I have tested software, run IT for a retreat center in Vermont, and answered support tickets, and I still cannot leave a repetitive task alone. I am also a creative: I write and play music, make video, and at CreatorIQ I wrote, designed, animated, recorded, and mixed the work myself. The two have run side by side the whole way. When they line up, I build things like an intake system that keeps creative work from getting lost, or a pipeline that cuts footage while I am with my family. When they do not, I am the one in the room who speaks both languages.

Where I Live

I first heard Steve Jobs describe Apple as living at the intersection of the arts and technology, and I have been trying to live there ever since.

I can't just write the music. I need to produce it and build the gear that captures it. I can't just edit video or pitch ideas. I need to build the pipeline that saves the hours, so I can spend them with my family and still publish on a steady cadence.

I have onboarded, trained, and mentored new team members. I lead with care, kindness, and a sense of humor, because people do their best work when they feel looked after.
