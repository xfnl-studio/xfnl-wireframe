About

Started in tech support. Learned to tell stories. Now I build the systems behind them.

I studied digital filmmaking at the Art Institute of Philadelphia. My first job at BigCommerce was answering support tickets. Then I moved into video, producing for the marketing team, and then into program management, where I've spent my time making creative work easier to ship.

Where I live

I first heard Steve Jobs describe Apple as living at the intersection of the arts and technology, and I have been trying to live there ever since.

I can't just write the music. I need to produce it and build the gear that captures it. I can't just edit video or pitch ideas. I need to build the pipeline that saves the hours, so I can spend them with my family and still publish on a steady cadence.

I'm a project manager who leads with care, kindness, and a sense of humor, because people do their best work when they feel looked after.
