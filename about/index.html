About

Cross-Functional by Nature, Nerd by Choice.

I've always lived in two worlds at once. I'm the nerd who ran IT for a retreat center in Vermont, tested software, and answered support tickets, and I still can't leave a repetitive task alone. I'm also a maker: I write and play music, I've produced video for years, and I love a good pipeline. The two have run side by side the whole way. When they line up, I build things like an Asana setup that keeps creative work from getting lost, or a pipeline that cuts footage while I'm at the park with my family. When they don't, I'm the one in the room who speaks both languages.

Where I Live

When I was a kid, I heard Steve Jobs describe Apple as sitting at the intersection of arts and technology, and I've tried to live there ever since.

I can't just write music. I need to develop concepts, produce them, and collect the gear that records it. I can't just edit video. I need to pitch ideas and build the pipeline that saves hours of my time, so I can play with my family at the park, and still publish on a steady cadence!

I've onboarded, trained, and mentored new team members. I lead with care, kindness, and a sense of humor, because people do their best work when they feel looked after.
