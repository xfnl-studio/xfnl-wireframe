03What I Build

Explore the Lab →

xfnl Studio is my personal cross-functional (xfnl) production and program management brand. With AI-assisted development (vibe coding), I designed and built Waves, a local LLM inference stack. Clarity, my proactive personal agent, ties it together and makes the three-machine, four-lane setup friendly to talk to. I also designed and vibe-coded the Automated Post-Production Assistant (APPA), a junior editor that takes about 15 hours a week (estimate) off my plate.

WavesSystem

A local LLM inference stack I designed and built with AI-assisted development (vibe coding). Three machines, four compute lanes (Thinking, Seeing, Remembering, and Embedding), and no cloud dependency.

ClarityAgent

My proactive personal agent. She ties the three-machine, four-lane setup together, routes tasks to Asana, and kicks off post-production jobs from a single Siri voice prompt.

APPAPipeline

An AI junior-editor pipeline I designed and vibe-coded. It transcribes, grades, and assembles multi-camera shoots into Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve timelines, taking about 15 hours a week (estimate) off my plate.

The craft and the tools keep meeting in the same places.
