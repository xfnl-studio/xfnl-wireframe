Family Sync & The 2.9x Asana Intake OS

When we migrated 50 marketers and creatives across BigCommerce, Feedonomics, and Makeswift from Jira to Asana, downstream designers and web publishers were losing Figma links and copy docs across subtasks. Vendor reps told us native cross-level asset syncing was impossible without expensive third-party middleware.

I sat down with Claude Code and the Asana v3 Node.js SDK and built Family Sync: an idempotent dependency engine that automatically propagates Final Figma Links, Final Copy Docs, and Final Image Assets across parent and sibling subtasks, handles CLEAR-keyword resets, and fires blocked-status alerts. Paired with a live 33-person automation keynote and the revived mTalks series, quarterly deliverable throughput climbed 2.9x (419 to 1,213), brief compliance jumped from 0% to 39% in 30 days, escalations dropped from 14.5% to 9.0%, and median cycle time compressed from ~30 to ~22 days.
